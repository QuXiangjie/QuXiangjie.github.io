About Jack Qu

I'm Jack Qu, an AI Engineer who turns messy business processes into production agentic systems. What drives me is the business side — finding a slow, manual workflow and rebuilding it as an LLM agent that does the work end-to-end. At an EdTech company I built a multi-agent RFP platform on the Claude Agent SDK (discovery → product matching with MCP + RAG → proposal → a real-time voice agent) and a production education-standards platform on AWS — surfacing 4× more qualified opportunities and lifting win rate.
